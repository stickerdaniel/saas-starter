## Customer contact

All optional:

- **Welcome email:** from a named person, once a new account verifies its email address. Replies reach that person.
- **Feedback request:** to accounts that have not paid, about 48 hours after verification.
- **Cancellation email:** asks customers who cancel a paid plan what was missing. Ouch, but useful.
- **Support chat:** answers right away and fetches a person when it runs out of ideas.
- **Incident email:** after a production bug is fixed, the accounts it hit can hear about it.
