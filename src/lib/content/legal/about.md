## How we work with customers

We ship small releases often and shape them around what customers tell us. Each step of that loop reaches customers directly once it is switched on:

- **A person writes to you.** With the welcome email on, new accounts get a personal welcome email as soon as the email address is verified. Your reply reaches the person who sent it.
- **We ask what is missing.** With feedback on, an account that has not paid about 48 hours after its email address is verified gets a feedback request from the same person. With the cancellation email on, customers who cancel a paid plan are asked what was missing.
- **Support hands off to a person.** The support chat answers right away and passes the conversation on when it needs a human.
- **We tell you about bugs that hit you.** When our records show that a production bug affected your account, we can email you after the fix is live.
