## How we work with customers

We ship small releases often and shape them around what customers tell us. Each step of that loop reaches you directly:

- **A person writes to you.** New accounts get a personal welcome email from one person on our team as soon as the email address is verified. Your reply reaches that person.
- **We ask what is missing.** If you have not paid 48 hours after signup, the same person asks for your feedback. If you cancel your subscription, we ask what was missing.
- **Support hands off to people.** The support chat answers right away and passes the conversation to our team when it needs a person.
- **We tell you about bugs that hit you.** When our records show that a production bug affected your account, we email you after the fix is live.
